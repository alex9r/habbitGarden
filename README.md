# Habit Garden 🪴

Habit Garden is a playful, child-friendly habit tracking app where your consistency grows a living digital garden.

Each habit you create becomes a plant — a flower, crop, or tree — that grows when you complete your daily habits and slowly wilts when you don’t. By filling out a short daily check-in, you nurture your garden and visually see the impact of your discipline over time.

Miss too many days? Your plant moves to the Habit Graveyard — a reflective space where you can revisit abandoned habits and revive them by writing a promise to yourself.

## ✨ Features

- 🌼 Habits represented as animated plants  
- 🌱 Growth stages based on consistency  
- 🌧 Visual decay when habits are neglected  
- 🪦 Habit Graveyard for reflection and revival  
- 🎨 Bright, child-friendly, animated UI  
- 📅 Simple once-daily habit check-in system  

## 🎯 Goal

To turn habit tracking into an emotionally engaging, visual experience that encourages consistency through creativity and gentle accountability.
